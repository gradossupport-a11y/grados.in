The Story

GradOS began as a personal project during my own graduate application journey. Like many applicants, I found myself managing university applications, supervisor emails, recommendation letters, funding opportunities, deadlines, research ideas, and countless documents spread across spreadsheets, email threads, notebooks, and browser bookmarks.



As the number of applications grew, staying organized became just as challenging as preparing the applications themselves. I searched for a tool designed specifically for graduate applicants, but couldn't find one that combined application tracking, supervisor outreach, referee management, deadline reminders, research notes, and opportunity discovery in one place. So I decided to build GradOS.
